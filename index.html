Reading the Tree

How it works: In Matula's bijection, 1 maps to a single node. A prime pk (the k-th prime) maps to a root connected to the tree for k. A composite n = a × b × c… maps to a root whose children are the trees for a, b, c… Every natural number produces a unique tree. Every rooted tree has a unique number.
